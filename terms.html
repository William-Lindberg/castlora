2. A free service without accounts

Castlora is free to use. There is no subscription and there are no ads. You don't need an account: Castlora runs on your device and stores your data there (see the privacy policy).

We may change, add or remove features, or stop offering Castlora, at any time. Because your data is stored on your device, you can always export it from Settings → Export while you still have the app.

3. Availability

We don't guarantee that Castlora, or any part of it, will always be available, work without errors, or work on every device or browser. The website and downloads are hosted by GitHub, and imports depend on Chess.com's public API. Both are outside our control and can be slow, limited or unavailable.

4. Fair play

Castlora is made for learning from games after they have finished.

  • Only analyse completed games.
  • Do not use Castlora, or anything it shows you, to get help during a game in progress on any site or app. That includes live, daily and correspondence games, and over-the-board games.
  • Follow the fair play rules of the sites and events where you play.

Castlora imports finished games only and never offers live-game assistance. If you break these rules on another site, that site may close your account. That is your responsibility.

5. Content you import

You are responsible for the content you bring into Castlora, such as PGN files and the usernames you import, and for how you use it. Chess.com's public API lets you import the public games of any username. Don't use that to harass anyone, to prepare unfairly against an opponent during an event where this is not allowed, or for any other unfair or unlawful purpose.

Your data is stored only on your device. You are responsible for keeping backups (Settings → Export). Your browser or device can delete stored data, and we can't recover it for you.
