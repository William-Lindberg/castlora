Privacy policy

Castlora is built so that your data never reaches us. Here is exactly what happens.

Last updated 6 November 2026No data collected

In short

  • Castlora has no accounts and no servers. The developer receives no personal data.
  • Your games, analysis and training data are stored only on your device.
  • No cookies, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
  • Your device only contacts GitHub, to load Castlora, and Chess.com, when you import games by username.

1. Who makes Castlora

Castlora is made by William Matthew Lindberg ("the developer", "we"). This policy covers the Castlora website, the web app and the Android app.

2. What the developer collects

Nothing. The developer runs no servers for Castlora and has no accounts system. We don't receive, see or store your games, your usernames, your analysis or any other personal data.

Castlora uses no cookies, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The website and the app load no third-party scripts, fonts or embeds.

3. What is stored on your device

To work, Castlora stores the following only on your device:

  • the games you import: moves, player names or usernames, ratings, dates and results, as provided by Chess.com or contained in your PGN files;
  • the engine analysis of those games, the mistakes found, and your training schedule and history;
  • your played and prepared opening repertoires;
  • your settings, including the Chess.com username you entered and your level.

In the web app, this data is kept in your browser's storage (IndexedDB) for this website, in that browser on that device. In the Android app, it is kept in the app's private storage. The data is not synced between devices and is not uploaded anywhere. Analysis runs on your device with the Stockfish engine.

4. Network requests

Castlora makes only these network requests:

a. Loading the website and web app (GitHub Pages)

The website and the web app files are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical information such as your IP address when your browser requests these files. GitHub is responsible for that processing; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement. The Android app includes its own copy of the app files, so it doesn't need to load them from GitHub.

b. Importing from Chess.com

When you import games by username, your device contacts api.chess.com directly and sends the username you entered. Chess.com receives that request together with technical information such as your IP address, and Chess.com's privacy policy applies. Castlora doesn't send Chess.com anything else, and the request doesn't pass through any Castlora server. The web app's security policy only allows connections to its own website and to api.chess.com.

You can import the public games of any Chess.com username. Please don't import other people's games for unfair purposes (see the terms of use).

c. Downloading the Android app (GitHub Releases)

The APK is downloaded from GitHub Releases, so GitHub's privacy statement applies to that download. The Android app asks only for the Internet permission, which it needs to import games from Chess.com.

Links to other sites, such as GitHub or Chess.com, open those sites in your browser. Their own privacy policies apply there.

5. Export and delete your data

  • Export: Settings → Export saves all your Castlora data as a JSON backup file. You can import it again later or on another device.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete all data removes everything Castlora has stored on the device.
  • Uninstalling the Android app, or clearing this website's data in your browser, also deletes your Castlora data.
  • Backup and export files you saved are ordinary files outside Castlora (in the web app, wherever your browser saves downloads; in the Android app, usually your Documents folder). Deleting your data or uninstalling the app does not remove them, so delete them yourself if you no longer need them.

Since we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. You control all of it on your device.

6. Legal basis and GDPR

Because the developer collects no personal data through Castlora, there is no processing of personal data by the developer as a controller for Castlora itself. Data stored on your device stays under your control. GitHub (hosting and downloads) and Chess.com (game imports) are independent controllers for their own services, and their privacy policies explain your rights towards them.

7. Children

Castlora collects no personal data from anyone, including children. Chess.com and GitHub set their own age requirements for their services. Younger children should use Castlora with a parent or guardian.

8. Security

Your Castlora data is protected by the security of your device, your browser and the Android system, so keep them up to date and locked. Browsers can clear stored website data, for example when storage is low, in private windows, or when you clear your browsing data. Make regular backups with Settings → Export. Backup files are not encrypted, so store them somewhere safe.

9. Changes to this policy

If Castlora changes in a way that affects your privacy, this policy will be updated first, and the date at the top will change. Significant changes will be noted on this page.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact the developer through github.com/William-Lindberg or open an issue on the public Castlora repository. Issues are public, so please don't include personal information in them.
