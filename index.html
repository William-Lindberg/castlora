How it works

From finished game to focused training

Three steps. Analysis and training happen on your device: no upload, no waiting for a server, no account to create.

  1. 1

    Import your games

    Enter a Chess.com username to fetch your finished games from Chess.com's public API, or open PGN files exported from any site.

Features

Seven tools, one source: your own games

Castlora doesn't give you generic puzzles. Every exercise and every insight comes from games you played.

Game analysis

Every move of your finished games is checked by Stockfish 19 on your device. You get an evaluation graph, the best moves and the moments where the game turned.

Mistake intelligence

Errors are graded and sorted by theme, so you see why you lose points. Retry each position, with hints when you need them.

Privacy

Your chess stays on your device

Castlora is local-first. There is no Castlora account and no Castlora server. Your games, analysis and training history are stored on your device and nowhere else.

  • No account. Open the app and start. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • Nothing is sent to us. The developer runs no servers for Castlora and receives none of your data.
  • No ads, analytics, tracking or cookies.
  • You stay in control. Export a JSON backup or delete everything from Settings at any time.

FAQ

Honest answers

Why is my accuracy different from Chess.com's?

Castlora and Chess.com use different engine settings and different formulas. Castlora runs Stockfish 19 lite on your own device, with depth limits that suit a phone or laptop. Chess.com runs its own analysis on its own servers. So the same game can get a different accuracy score, and sometimes a different verdict on a move.

All engine evaluations and accuracy numbers are estimates. They're most useful when you compare Castlora's numbers with each other over time.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. The web app stores it in your browser's storage (IndexedDB), and the Android app in its own app storage. Nothing is synced, so your data in one browser is not visible in another browser or on another device.

To move or back up your data, use Settings → Export to save a JSON file, and import it on the other device. Settings → Delete all data removes everything Castlora stores on that device. Backup files you exported are ordinary files: delete them yourself if you no longer need them.

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no subscription, no ads and no tracking. Castlora doesn't need servers because everything runs on your device, so there are no running costs to pass on to you.

Can I use Castlora during a game?

No. Castlora only imports and analyses completed games and never offers help during a live game. Using an engine or analysis tool during a game in progress, including daily or correspondence games, breaks the fair play rules of Chess.com and other sites.

Do I need a Chess.com account?

No. To import from Chess.com you only type a username. Castlora fetches that player's public, finished games from Chess.com's public API. You can also open PGN files, for example games exported from Lichess or from another chess app. You don't need a Castlora account either.

How do I install the Android app from GitHub?
  1. On your phone, open the latest release on GitHub and download the Castlora .apk file.
  2. Recommended: check that the file's SHA-256 checksum matches the one in SHA256SUMS.txt in the same release.
  3. Open the APK. Android asks you to allow your browser or file manager to install unknown apps. Allow it for that app only.
  4. Install Castlora. Afterwards you can switch the "install unknown apps" permission off again.

Google is rolling out Android developer verification for apps installed outside Google Play, so depending on your device and country, Android may show extra warnings or steps. Only download Castlora from the official release page.

How does Castlora decide my level?

After you import from Chess.com, it uses your Chess.com Rapid rating. Without enough Rapid games it uses another Chess.com time class as a provisional level, and without any rating you pick your level yourself. You can change it at any time. The levels are Beginner below 1000, Intermediate from 1000 to 1799, and Advanced from 1800. These are Castlora's own, provisional categories for pitching explanations and training. They are not official titles or ratings.

Will analysis slow down my device?

Analysis runs on your device's processor, so speed depends on your hardware. A phone takes longer than a laptop to analyse many games. Castlora uses the lite, single-threaded build of Stockfish 19 to keep things light.

Is Castlora open source?

No. Castlora is free to use, but it is proprietary software: the source code isn't published, and you may not copy, modify or redistribute it. See the Castlora License.

The Stockfish chess engine inside Castlora is a separate program licensed under the GNU GPL v3. Its complete source code is public, and your GPL rights for those files are not restricted. All third-party licences are listed under open-source licences and notices.

Is Castlora affiliated with Chess.com?

No. Castlora is an independent project and is not affiliated with Chess.com, Lichess or the Stockfish project. It uses Chess.com's public API under Chess.com's terms.
